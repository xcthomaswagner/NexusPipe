# GEO Best Practices and Methodologies

## Source: Profound's 10-Step Framework (2025)

### Comprehensive GEO Framework

#### Step 1: Align GEO Objectives With Business KPIs
- Map AI visibility to real business impact
- Identify primary metrics: leads, revenue, signups
- Add "How did you hear about us?" to forms
- **Key KPIs to Track**:
  - Revenue Growth: AI-attributed leads (+20% YoY target)
  - Brand Awareness: Visibility score (Top 3 target)
  - Customer Trust: Positive sentiment (≥90% favorable target)

#### Step 2: Audit Current AI Visibility and Sentiment
- Benchmark visibility score, share of voice, sentiment trends
- Analyze which AI platforms mention brand, frequency, and context
- Look for positive and negative sentiment patterns
- Identify current standing and improvement areas

#### Step 3: Map Real-User Prompts Across the Funnel
- Collect prompts from sales calls, customers, social listening (Reddit)
- Document actual questions and phrases at each journey stage
- Understand intent for both awareness and conversion queries
- Use tools to estimate LLM search volume from real user prompts
- Target 20-30 unique prompts per core topic

#### Step 4: Structure Content for AI-Friendly Summaries
- Incorporate TL;DR blocks, bullet lists, schema markup (FAQPage, HowTo)
- Use concise, well-organized summaries
- Prioritize clarity and brevity
- Use tables to organize information
- Prioritize comparative articles (Best X Thing, Comparing A to B)

#### Step 5: Optimize Technical Signals and Schema Markup
- HTTPS everywhere
- Mobile speed under 1.8 seconds
- Complete structured data coverage
- Track AI bot traffic to identify gaps
- Add LLMs.txt

#### Step 6: Elevate Citation Authority Through Thought Leadership
- Publish original research, whitepapers, expert commentary
- Create unique, data-driven content
- **Benchmark**: Earn citations from ≥20 high-authority domains per quarter

#### Step 7: Strengthen E-E-A-T and Brand Trust Signals
- Develop detailed author bios
- Transparent sourcing
- Review schemas
- Audit for outdated/low-trust content
- Include third-party reviews, certifications, clear contact info

#### Step 8: Integrate Multimedia and Data Assets
- Embed charts, infographics, video transcripts with descriptive captions
- Note: Most AI crawlers cannot parse JavaScript
- Use variety of media types
- Well-labeled multimedia assets with alt tags

#### Step 9: Scale Prompt Testing and Conversation Workflows
- Build library of iterative prompts
- Monitor responses and adapt to product/market changes
- Experiment with different question formats
- Run prompts daily for longitudinal visibility tracking

#### Step 10: Benchmark, Report, and Iterate Quarterly
- Follow consistent reporting cadence
- Track visibility, citation authority, sentiment
- Share results internally
- Set up competitive asset groups
- Review and update key prompts every 90 days

### Governance and Team Structure
- Typically led by Director of Digital Marketing or Head of SEO
- Partner with content and analytics teams for execution
- Share results with marketing team and leadership quarterly

## Key Differences: GEO vs Traditional SEO

### Traditional SEO Focus:
- Keyword rankings
- Backlink profiles
- Organic traffic
- Click-through rates

### GEO Focus:
- AI citation frequency
- Brand mention context
- Share of voice in AI responses
- Sentiment in AI-generated content
- Query coverage across AI platforms

## How AI Engines Evaluate Content

### Citation Factors:
1. Authority of source domain
2. Content structure and clarity
3. Schema markup and technical signals
4. E-E-A-T signals
5. Freshness and factual accuracy
6. Multimedia richness
7. Third-party validation

### Content Characteristics AI Prefers:
- Concise summaries
- Structured data
- Comparative analysis
- Data-driven insights
- Clear hierarchy (H2/H3)
- Paragraphs under 120 words
- Tables and lists
- Expert attribution
