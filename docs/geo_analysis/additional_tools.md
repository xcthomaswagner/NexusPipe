## Key Metrics Identified Across Tools

1. **AI Visibility Score**: Frequency of brand/content appearance in AI responses
2. **Source Citations**: Number of direct references or links to website
3. **Share of Voice**: Percentage of mentions vs competitors
4. **Sentiment Accuracy**: Whether AI describes brand positively
5. **Query Coverage**: Breadth of prompts/topics where brand is recognized
6. **Factual Alignment**: Accuracy of brand details shared by AI
