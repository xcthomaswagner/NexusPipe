# Top GEO Tools - Competitive Analysis

## Source: Alex Birkett Article (Dec 2025)

### Top 8 GEO Tools Identified

#### 1. Peec AI
- **Price**: €89/mo (25 prompts)
- **Focus**: Competitive intelligence + multi-model visibility tracking
- **Key Features**:
  - Tracks brand/competitors across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity
  - Benchmarking dashboard for competitor comparison
  - Citation and source data for outreach targeting
  - Shows which pages are commonly cited and whether brand is included

#### 2. Goodie AI
- **Price**: Free audit; contact for pricing
- **Focus**: All-in-one monitoring and optimization
- **Key Features**:
  - Unified dashboard across multiple AI engines
  - Content recommendations tuned for AI engines
  - Brand mention alerts and sentiment analysis
  - AI visibility hub with action steps
  - On-page LLM optimization guidance
  - Citation and page gaps analysis

#### 3. Profound
- **Price**: $499/mo (Lite), Enterprise custom pricing
- **Focus**: Enterprise command center for AI visibility
- **Key Features**:
  - Share of voice tracking across all major AI platforms
  - Agent Analytics: AI crawler behavior and attribution tracking
  - Conversation Explorer: real-time user query insights
  - ChatGPT Shopping visibility for products
  - Prompt clustering and trending
  - Sentiment scoring and message analysis
  - Dedicated strategist/Copilot support
- **Noted Issues**: High price, noisy interface, some features considered superfluous

#### 4. Ahrefs
- **Price**: $199/mo per index
- **Focus**: SEO powerhouse with GEO add-ons
- **Key Features**:
  - AI Keyword Suggestions and Content Helper
  - Brand Radar for visibility tracking
  - AI References feature
  - Deep backlink and content analytics
  - AI translations in 40+ languages
- **Noted Issues**: Expensive per-index pricing

#### 5. Semrush
- **Price**: $99/mo per domain (AI toolkit add-on), Enterprise custom
- **Focus**: Bridge traditional SEO and AI visibility
- **Key Features**:
  - Cross-platform AI visibility tracking
  - Share of voice and brand sentiment
  - Prompt rankings and benchmarking
  - Strategic recommendations from LLM insights
  - Enterprise AIO with real-time control
  - Alerting and sentiment analysis

#### 6. AthenaHQ
- **Price**: $270-295/mo (Lite), $545-900/mo (Growth), $2000+/mo (Enterprise)
- **Focus**: Fastest, most comprehensive GEO platform
- **Key Features**:
  - 3M+ response catalog mapping citations to 300K+ sites
  - Real-time visibility tracking
  - Action Center with recommendations (text, image, video)
  - Content gaps and sentiment analysis
  - Prompt triggers and SOV metrics
  - Competitor benchmarking
  - AI-powered optimization agents

#### 7. XFunnel
- **Price**: Custom pricing (acquired by HubSpot)
- **Focus**: Funnel-focused AI visibility + buyer journey optimization
- **Key Features**:
  - Buying Journey Analysis dashboard
  - Query insights by intent, region, product, persona
  - Optimization playbooks with expert support
  - Tracks visibility across awareness to decision stages

#### 8. Geostar
- **Price**: $299/mo
- **Focus**: Hybrid platform + managed services
- **Key Features**:
  - Visibility tracker
  - Crawler analytics
  - Managed GEO services with custom outreach

## Key Patterns Across Competitors

### Common Features:
1. Multi-platform AI engine tracking (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, etc.)
2. Competitor benchmarking and comparison
3. Sentiment analysis
4. Share of voice metrics
5. Citation source identification
6. Actionable recommendations

### Advanced Features (Enterprise tools):
1. AI crawler analytics
2. Real-time conversation/prompt trending
3. Dedicated strategist support
4. Buying journey mapping
5. Integration with traditional SEO tools
6. Custom outreach services
