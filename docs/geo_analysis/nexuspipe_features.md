# NexusPipe GEO App - Feature Summary

## Core Functionality
- **Focus**: Off-site GEO (brand visibility across the web in AI-relevant sources)
- **Does NOT do**: On-site GEO (website optimization for AI readability)

## Key Features

### 1. 4-Stage Pipeline
- **Discovery**: Neural semantic search to find AI-relevant sources
- **Ingestion**: Scrape and process discovered sources
- **Synthesis**: AI-powered analysis for brand mentions, competitors, sentiment
- **Results**: Calculate Visibility Index and identify opportunities

### 2. Visibility Metrics

#### Combined Visibility Score
- Blended metric combining web and AI visibility
- Formula: (Web Visibility × 60%) + (AI Share of Voice × 40%)
- The 60/40 weighting reflects that web citations currently drive more AI training data, but AI platform visibility is growing in importance

#### Web Visibility Index
- Formula: (Sources mentioning brand ÷ Total analyzed sources) × 100
- Measures brand presence across discovered web sources
- Scoring ranges: 0-10% (low) to 75%+ (dominant)

#### AI Share of Voice
- Formula: (AI responses mentioning brand ÷ Total AI responses) × 100
- Measures how often AI platforms mention your brand in responses
- Based on test queries run against ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and other AI platforms

### 3. Citation Gap Analysis
- Identifies high-authority sources mentioning competitors but not the brand
- Provides personalized target list for outreach

### 4. Sentiment Analysis
- Categories: Positive, Neutral, Mixed
- Analyzes tone and context of brand mentions

### 5. Scan Depth Options
- Quick (25 sources, ~2 min)
- Standard (50 sources, ~5 min)
- Deep (100 sources, ~10 min)

### 6. Industry Intent Query
- Natural language question guiding discovery
- Simulates how users ask AI about the industry

### 7. Exclusion Management
- Auto-excludes brand's own website and competitor websites
- Manual exclusion options for social media, job boards, etc.

### 8. Competitor Tracking
- Optional competitor comparison
- Enables citation gap analysis

## Use Cases
- Brand awareness measurement in AI responses
- Competitive positioning analysis
- Content strategy alignment
- PR and outreach prioritization
